What we make

Joinery, built for the room it goes in.

Each job starts with the space and the way it is used. The workshop then builds to that, rather than asking the room to accept a standard size.

Bespoke kitchens

Kitchens made as one-off pieces, including storage that matches how the room is actually used. Cabinets are made to the walls you have, and tired kitchens can be refurbished instead of replaced.

Doors and windows

Wooden doors and windows in hardwood or softwood, single or double glazed. That includes sash windows, arched openings, internal doors, and curved shop fronts.

Furniture and restoration

Tables, dressers, bedroom pieces, and the odd one-off for a wedding or a gift. Older furniture can be restored or brought back into daily use.

Stairways

A tired staircase can be made fresh for less than a full replacement. New stairways are built too, with glass, timber spindles, or a simpler modern rail.

Outdoor work

Gates, garden furniture, garages, summer houses, and shepherd huts. The same workshop, built for the weather.

Commercial projects

Bars, counters, shop fronts, surgeries, and office refits. Made-to-measure joinery, with the job run so the business is disrupted as little as possible.

Churches and chapels

Doors, pews, vestry fittings, wall pieces, and pedestals for churches and chapels across Wales.

Listed buildings

Much of the work is in Grade I and Grade II buildings. Hand-carved mouldings and traditional leaded lights are made to sit with what was already there.
